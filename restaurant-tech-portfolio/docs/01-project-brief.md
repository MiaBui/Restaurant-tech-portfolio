# Project brief — draft
## Business context
Fictional Asian brunch café: 12 tables, dine-in and takeout, kitchen and beverage station. Proposed equipment: two order terminals, one kitchen printer, one beverage printer. Actual compatibility and network requirements remain unverified.
## Problem
Design clear order capture and routing, including required choices and paid additions, and prepare staff for opening day.
## Scope
Requirements, menu design, device planning, test design, training, go-live readiness, and escalation documentation.
## Out of scope
Live payment processing, production network changes, actual Toast configuration, and tax/legal configuration.
## Stakeholders (fictional roles)
Owner approves scope; manager approves workflows; kitchen/bar validate routing; servers validate usability; implementation specialist documents and coordinates.
## Proposed success criteria
Every requirement has an acceptance criterion and a linked test; all launch-blocking tests pass in an authorized test environment before go-live. No results are claimed in this simulation.
## Open questions for the author
1. Why 12 tables? Which service workflow does this imply?
2. Who may void or refund transactions?
3. What is the approved response if a kitchen printer fails?
4. What device/network information would you request before installation?
5. Who makes the final go/no-go decision?

# Staff training — draft
Audience: new servers. Objective: enter and verify a dine-in order with required modifiers.

## Five-minute session
- Explain the order type, table, and item choices.
- Demonstrate Banh Mi with a required protein and optional extra protein using the menu specification.
- Ask the learner to repeat the task and explain the final price before tax.
- Ask: what happens if no protein is selected? Who can authorize a void?
- Close with where to find help.
